# Hi, I'm Matthias 👋

I work at the intersection of **Public Key Infrastructure**, **Identity & Access Management**, **cryptography**, **key and secrets management**, and **enterprise security architecture**.

My focus is on designing and implementing trusted digital identity infrastructures for complex environments — from traditional enterprise IT to cloud, hybrid, and operational technology environments.

---

## 🔐 Areas I work with

- **Public Key Infrastructure**
  - Enterprise PKI architecture and design
  - Certificate lifecycle management
  - Certificate policies, trust models, revocation concepts, and enrollment workflows
  - Device, user, service, application, and machine identity use cases

- **Identity & Access Management**
  - Enterprise identity architectures
  - Hybrid identity and authentication models
  - Certificate-based authentication
  - Role models, access governance, and security boundaries

- **Key & Secrets Management**
  - Key lifecycle concepts
  - Secrets management and secure credential handling
  - Hardware-backed key protection and trust anchors
  - Encryption, signing, and secure service integration patterns

- **Enterprise & OT Security**
  - Security architecture and governance
  - Privileged access and tiering concepts
  - Device identity and trust in industrial environments
  - Migration, consolidation, and operating models for security-critical infrastructure

---

## 🧭 What I care about

I like building security solutions that are:

- understandable for operations teams,
- robust enough up to enterprise scale,
- realistic to implement,
- maintainable over time,
- and documented well enough to survive audits, migrations, and organizational change.

For me, good security architecture is not only about technology.

It is also about governance, ownership, maintainability, operating models, and clear processes.
